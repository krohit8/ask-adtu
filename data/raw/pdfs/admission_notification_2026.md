PhD Admission– 2026 -27
Assam down town University (AdtU) stands as one of the leading universities in North East India, which
offers students a unique blend of academic excellence and research opportunities. By combining the
expertise of eminent academicians, highly motivated teachers, and top industry leaders, AdtU has
created an environment that nurtures teaching, learning, and experiential growth. This approach
equips students with a global perspective and instils a positive attitude towards embracing challenges.
As one of the largest educational groups in Assam, AdtU boasts an impressive student body of over
8000 students and a faculty team of more than 400 members. The university is situated on a sprawling
50- acres campus nestled amidst picturesque green hills. Recognizing the importance of research,
AdtU consistently upgrades its state-of-the-art laboratories and research facilities, thereby promoting
and encouraging a wide range of research activities. The university's commitment to academic and
research excellence, combined with its expansive campus and dedicated faculty, provides students
with a conducive learning environment that fosters holistic development. AdtU strives to empower its
students with the necessary knowledge, skills, and mindset to navigate the challenges of the real world
successfully.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR ADMISSION TO Ph.D PROGRAMME:
Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Ph.D Programme:
Candidates who have completed:
• A 1-year/2-semester master's degree programme after a 4-year/8-semester bachelor’s
degree programme or a 2-year/4-semester master’s degree programme after a 3-year
bachelor’s degree programme or qualifications declared equivalent to the master’s degree by
the corresponding statutory regulatory body, with at least 55% marks in aggregate or its
equivalent grade in a point scale wherever grading system is followed or equivalent
qualification from a foreign educational institution accredited by an assessment and
accreditation agency which is approved, recognized or authorized by an authority, established
or incorporated under a law in its home country or any other statutory authority in that
country to assess, accredit or assure quality and standards of the educational institution. A
relaxation of 5% marks or its equivalent grade may be allowed for those belonging to
SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer)/Differently-Abled, Economically Weaker Section (EWS) and
other categories of candidates as per the decision of the Commission from time to time.

AdtU-RET:
AdtU-RET examination has the following two components:
• Written test [70marks]
• Interview/Personal Interaction [30 marks]
Components of PhD–RET Written Examination:
Sl. No Name of the sub-components Marks
(i) Subject Specific Questions 35
(ii) Research Methodology 35
Interview/Personal interaction: Students who have secured 50% marks in the written test will be
eligible for the interview.
Exemption from Entrance test:
• The candidates who have qualified for fellowship/scholarship in UGC-NET/UGC- CSIR
NET/GATE/ GPAT/CEED and similar National level tests based on an interview in the
concerned subject are exempted from the written examination of AdtU-RET. However, they
shall have to appear in interview/personal interaction for getting admission into the program.
• NET qualified candidates are exempted from written examination.NET score obtained by the
candidates in NET examination will be utilized for PhD admission as per UGC guidelines.
However, such candidates shall have to appear in interview /personal interaction for getting
admission into the programme.
Concept Note and PPT:
• All the selected applicants need to submit a concept note on their research interest/area (2-3
pages) and power point (PPT) presentation. Submission of concept note & PPT is mandatory
before interview/presentation.
• Candidates are required to present and discuss their research interest/area before the
selection panel.
