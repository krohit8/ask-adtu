Phd Entrance for the Faculty of Nursing (2026)
Assam down town University (AdtU) stands as one of the leading universities in North East
India, which offers students a unique blend of academic excellence and research opportunities.
By combining the expertise of eminent academicians, highly motivated teachers, and top
industry leaders, AdtU has created an environment that nurtures teaching, learning, and
experiential growth. This approach equips students with a global perspective and instills a
positive attitude towards embracing challenges. As one of the largest educational groups in
Assam, AdtU boasts an impressive student body of over 8000 students and a faculty team of
more than 400 members. The university is situated on a sprawling 50- acres campus nestled
amidst picturesque green hills. Recognizing the importance of research, AdtU consistently
upgrades its state-of-the-art laboratories and research facilities, thereby promoting and
encouraging a wide range of research activities. The university's commitment to academic and
research excellence, combined with its expansive campus and dedicated faculty, provides
students with a conducive learning environment that fosters holistic development. AdtU strives
to empower its students with the necessary knowledge, skills, and mindset to navigate the
challenges of the real world successfully.

Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Ph.D Programme (only for Faculty of Nursing):
Applications are invited from eligible scholars who have obtained:
a. M.Phil. (Nursing) or M.Sc. (Nursing) or a Post Graduate degree in Nursing recognized by
INC Council with 3 years teaching or Clinical experience after M.Sc. (Nursing) for
enrolment for Ph.D in Nursing (part-time) under Faculty of Nursing in the respective
University.
b. Scholars should have passed M.Sc. (Nursing) with a minimum of 60% marks.
