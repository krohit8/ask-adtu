The SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is calculated as
SGPA= ƩCiGi/ ƩCi
Where, Ci is the number of credits of the ith course and Gi is the grade point scored by the student in the ith course.
At the end of the programme, The CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is calculated as
CGPA= ƩCnGn/ ƩCn
Where, Gn is the SGPA of the nth Semester and Cn is the total number of credits in that Semester.
Conversion of CGPA in to Percentage= (CGPA×10)
